FREQUENTLY ASKED

What does J's Bite serve? +
J's Bite serves halal smashed beef burgers, crispy chicken burgers, wings, loaded fries, sandwiches and sides in Dahye–Rweis, Lebanon — for Dine In, Take Away or Delivery.
Where are you located? +
We're in Dahye – Rweis, Beirut, Lebanon. Tap "Find Us" or message us on WhatsApp for exact directions.
Is the food halal? +
Yes — everything on our menu is 100% halal.
Do you deliver? +
Yes. We offer Dine In, Take Away and Delivery. Place your order on WhatsApp at 79 016 397 and we'll bring it to your door.
How do I order? +
Browse the menu, tap + to add items to your cart, then send the order to us on WhatsApp — or message us directly at 79 016 397.
What are your opening hours? +
We're open every day from 1:00 PM to 2:00 AM.
Best value for a group? +
Our family meals are built for sharing — Family Chicken Meal ($30) and Family Beef Meal ($40). Not sure? Use "Don't know what to eat?" above the menu.
Any spicy options? +
Plenty — the Spicy Storm chicken burger, the Z Crunchy, the J's Prime beef burger, and our jalapeño bites bring the heat.
What should I try first? +
Start with a signature burger and a side of jalapeño bites — a staff favorite, they're fire. And our frying oil is changed daily, so everything comes out clean and crisp.
How much does it cost? +
Sides start at $3.50, burgers range from $7 to $11, and combo offers start at $10.
